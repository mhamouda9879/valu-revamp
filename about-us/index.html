Our story

Your Financial Compass

Est. December 2017EGX listedEgypt & Jordan

Valu is Egypt’s leading universal financial technology powerhouse, dedicated to delivering a comprehensive suite of financial solutions to both individuals and businesses. Since our launch in Egypt in December 2017, we have remained committed to providing seamless, accessible, and tailored services that meet our customers' evolving needs. In 2026, Valu expanded its regional footprint with the launch in Jordan, marking a significant milestone in our growth journey and reinforcing our vision to redefine financial experiences through our financing solutions.

A first for EgyptValu is the first fintech company providing consumer finance to become a listed company on the Egyptian Exchange (EGX), and this represents Valu’s growth journey and dynamic business model.

At Valu, we understand the complexities of navigating the financial world, and we strive to be your trusted partner every step of the way. Our team of passionate advisors is dedicated to helping you achieve your financial goals with confidence and ease.
